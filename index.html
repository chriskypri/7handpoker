♠ How to play

Explore the steps using Back and Next before you challenge a friend.

  1. Start a match. Enter your name and create a private room. Share its link or QR code with a friend, or join using their room code.
  2. Choose your cards. Each player starts with 7 cards. On your turn, select 1 to 5 cards and press Play selected cards.
  3. Place the hidden hand. Your opponent sees how many cards you played, but not their values, and chooses one of the seven coin positions for them. You do the same when they play.
  4. Compete for coins. When both players have cards at a position, the stronger poker combination wins that coin. The game draws 3 replacement cards after each submitted hand.
  5. Win the match. Be the first to claim 4 coins in total or 3 consecutive coins.
🃏 Poker hands — strongest to weakest
1. Royal flush10–J–Q–K–A, same suit (highest straight flush)
2. Straight flushFive consecutive cards, same suit
3. Four of a kindFour cards of the same rank
4. Full houseThree of a kind plus a pair
5. FlushFive cards of the same suit
6. StraightFive consecutive ranks
7. Three of a kindThree cards of the same rank
8. Two pairTwo different pairs
9. One pairTwo cards of the same rank
10. High cardHighest card wins if no combination

Royal flush is the highest straight flush. You can play fewer than five cards, but combinations needing five cards won't be possible.
